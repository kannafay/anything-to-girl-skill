# Everything to Anime Girl

Translate the source's identity into a beautiful original adult anime woman. Explicit user choices override creative defaults.

## Understand the request

- Use the current source and its visible features. A new object or image means a new character; preserve identity and unmentioned details when revising an existing character.
- Distinguish source/identity images from style references and rejected examples. Borrow rendering from a style reference while keeping the requested subject and composition. Treat source facts separately from invented character traits.
- Choose creative defaults yourself. Ask only when essential source information is missing. For concept, analysis or prompt-only requests, give text; for image requests, use an available image-generation tool.

## Design priorities

- **Recognizability:** choose the source's most distinctive colors, shapes, materials or functions and translate them naturally into the character. Let the source guide construction and silhouette rather than relying on repeated logos or a held object. No fixed number of cues or mandatory placement is needed.
- **Beauty:** create an attractive adult woman with harmonious anime features, expressive eyes, an engaging expression, believable anatomy and a graceful pose. Vary faces, hair and silhouettes across unrelated sources.
- **Fashion:** design flattering, sophisticated, moderately sensual adult fantasy clothing. Sleek mature glamour and ornate sweet glamour are compatible directions; choose or blend them to suit the source. Cuts, skin exposure, layers and accessories are creative choices, guided by the user's preferences. Source function can inspire fashion without prescribing a work uniform.
- **Finish:** reference Genshin Impact's official 2D character-illustration aesthetics for character and fashion appeal. Combine fine integrated colored linework with nuanced layered shading. Refine facial features, hair and visible garment construction; make materials distinct and accessories convincingly crafted. Concentrate detail around focal points, with quieter areas for balance. Keep the design original and follow any user-specified style.

Read [design-language.md](references/design-language.md) when source translation, wardrobe variety or the level of finish needs more guidance. Its examples are optional inspiration, not required ingredients.

## Output format

- One character shown once in a standalone full-body composition, with the entire head and both feet visible. Default to a vertical image, a quiet pale background and a subtle contact shadow.
- No text anywhere, including clothes and props. No panels, inset portraits, extra views, turnarounds or collages. A style reference's layout does not change this format.

## Compose, generate and deliver

- Write a compact English production prompt containing the current source anchors, aesthetic goal, chosen design details and essential output constraints. Keep planning instructions, unused options, example menus and repeated prohibitions out of it. Use [image-prompts.md](references/image-prompts.md) as a starting point, adapting it to the request.
- Attach relevant user-supplied images when supported and identify each image's role. Generate the requested image count, defaulting to one. Keep replies brief and in the user's language; avoid duplicating an image already displayed by the tool.
- When viewable, check source recognizability, facial beauty, coherent fashion, refined finish, anatomy, full-body framing and absence of text or extra views. Correct an obvious issue with a focused edit rather than redesigning unrelated parts. If generation is unavailable, explain the limitation.
