# Design language: from thing to anime woman

Read this reference when the source's unique traits are hard to translate, or when multiple designs are becoming too similar. This is a design framework, not a list of costumes to copy.

## A. The recognizability ladder

1. **Literal object:** She holds the object, wears its printed name, or has its logo pasted on a chest plate. Weak.
2. **Decorative inspiration:** The object inspires colors, but the shape and function are missing. Moderate.
3. **Structural reinterpretation:** Its unique silhouette appears in hairstyle/clothing construction, its material in fabrics, and its mechanism in details. Strong.
4. **Character-native design:** The object's color/shape/function jointly determine the woman's distinct silhouette, accessories, role and temperament. Ideal.

Prefer levels 3-4. Avoid literalizing every component: 3-5 intelligently chosen anchors are stronger than 20 unrelated trinkets.

## B. Translation matrix by source type

| Source | Useful visual anchors | Strong character translation | Common error to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand/logo | Graphic geometry, emblem, limited palette, typographic personality | Hair ornament based on symbol; garment construction that echoes logo geometry; one subtle non-lettered emblem | Copy-pasting logo everywhere; claiming unsupported official personality |
| Food/drink | Shape, layers, ingredients, packaging, temperature, texture | Cream/foam hairstyle volume; leaf or straw hairpin; layered textile palette; mood linked to temperature | Costume looks like a walking package |
| Electronics | Ports, lenses, metal/glass, cable routes, compactness | Modular seams, translucent coat, concentric eyelet accessories, precise patterns | Generic cyberpunk armor unrelated to the device |
| Household items | Protection/storage/motion mechanism, hinges, handles, softness | Sleeve shape from handle or shade, hinged jewelry, portable tools, guardian/healer archetype | Only a hat that resembles the item |
| Weapons/tools | Balance, edge, grip, safety guards, speed | Sharp layered hair silhouette, structural collar or belt, skillful stance, restrained metal accents | Random decorative blades, impossible weapon anatomy |
| Transportation | Light signatures, body lines, proportions, tire/wing shapes | Streamlined coat, legwear, visor, aerodynamic hair pieces, dynamic yet readable silhouette | Simply posing beside a vehicle |
| Architecture | Arches, roofline, columns, texture, symmetry, historical details | Coat architecture, hair volume, ornament, jewelry and distinctive palette | Entire building strapped to the head |
| Plants/animals | Leaf, petal, horn, fins, wing or fur patterns | Shape-derived hair pins, soft collars, fin-like tails of cloth, characteristic eyes | Generic animal ears with no further translation |
| Abstract concepts | Representative shapes, metaphors, contrasts, rhythm | Symbolic geometry, gradients, asymmetrical fabric, role/ability tied to concept | Unrelated mystical girl with arbitrary colors |

## C. Distinctive source-to-character examples

These are independent examples, NOT simultaneous subjects. Use one source to design one character per image.

### 1. Orange radial AI logo
- Primary anchors: compact orange starburst / many tapered rays, warm terracotta, cream contrast.
- Character DNA: warm amber eyes; compact radial hair hardware and a source-shaped waist fastening; ivory, terracotta and charcoal color blocking; thoughtful, engaging adult femininity.
- Wardrobe options: a fitted asymmetric charcoal mini dress with a short draped ivory jacket and terracotta lining; or a structured ivory bodice, airy short layered skirt and a terracotta split train with fine pearl or amber accents. Choose one coherent design or blend selected details.
- Visual test: radial geometry expressed in tailoring and small coordinated accessories is stronger than repeating a large corporate emblem on every surface.

### 2. Black-and-white interlocking AI knot logo
- Primary anchors: overlapping loop structure, near-monochrome palette, continuous interconnection.
- Character DNA: charcoal-haired adult woman; interlocking metallic hairpiece; overlapping straps/seams; a fitted black outfit with an asymmetric hem and a cropped or draped white outer layer; calm, confident, curious energy.
- Distinction: do not imitate the orange logo's soft ruffles; use well-defined precise layers and a clean urban silhouette.

### 3. Blue whale-shaped technology logo
- Primary anchors: vivid cobalt blue, whale/water silhouette, white negative space, deep-sea metaphor.
- Character DNA: silver-white hair with ocean-blue highlights; a compact fin-inspired hairpin; wave-shaped neckline or skirt construction, flowing translucent fabric tails and blue glass detailing; elegant exploratory character.
- Distinction: emphasize fluid shapes, aquatic gradients and precise tech accessories instead of generic black futuristic armor.

### 4. Classic red aluminum soda can
- Primary anchors: cylindrical packaging, vivid red-white colors, pull ring, fizz, metallic texture.
- Character DNA: short energetic layered hair; red cropped sporty jacket over white asymmetrical skirt; pull-tab ear accessory; tiny bubble motifs in transparent cuffs; lively expression.

### 5. Black mirrorless camera
- Primary anchors: circular lens, rectangular body, shutter, textured grip.
- Character DNA: neat dark bob with circular metallic clasp; a cropped angular jacket over a fitted dress or tailored separates; iris-inspired brooch and concentric stitching; observant expression, city-photographer vibe.

### 6. Desk lamp
- Primary anchors: lamp shade curve, hinge, pool of light, matte metal.
- Character DNA: arched neckline and a short bell-shaped cape framing the shoulders; a defined waist and hinge-like cuff hardware; warm facial light; focused, quiet night-study persona.

## D. Anti-template checks

When producing multiple characters, independently choose all of these before image generation:

- Silhouette: fitted mini dress, layered short A-line, asymmetric separates, cropped jacket, tailored pants, sporty mini silhouette, or a long slit dress with a flowing train.
- Wardrobe balance: choose how neckline, shoulders, waist and legs contribute to the silhouette. Vary exposed and covered areas with the source and mood; make each outfit flattering and coordinated.
- Hair: short crop, bob, braid, pinned updo, mid-length waves, ponytail, long straight or twin-tail (based on source geometry).
- Temperament: playful, poised, composed, studious, mysterious, energetic or serene (based on source function), expressed through an attractive face, engaging eyes and a graceful pose.
- Material: matte knit, chiffon, polished metal, nylon, organza, velvet or satin (based on source texture).
- Shoe logic: boots, sneakers, loafers, platforms, heels or flats to match outfit/role, not automatically heels every time.

If two adjacent designs share too many of these despite very different objects, redesign one from the source before generating. A recurring, visually coherent illustration style is fine; repeated faces and outfits are not.
