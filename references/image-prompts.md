# Prompt construction

These are flexible scaffolds. Resolve every bracket before use. Apply the output and execution rules in SKILL.md, and include only the art preferences relevant to the current task.

## New personification

"Create one finished [STYLE] illustration of [SOURCE] as an adult woman, guided by [CHARACTER IDEA]. Give her expressive beauty and an individual, coherent design. Place her in a simply painted [PLACE / SITUATION], with selected environmental cues and room to breathe. [RELEVANT FIGURE PREFERENCES AND OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS]."

## Existing character

"Redraw [REFERENCE CHARACTER] in [STYLE]. Preserve [DEFINING IDENTITY AND DESIGN FEATURES], translating the whole figure and clothing shapes consistently. Change [REQUESTED SCOPE]. [SETTING IF NEEDED AND OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS]."

## Local revision

"Change [REQUESTED DETAIL] in [TARGET CHARACTER]. Preserve her identity, the intended style and unmentioned design. [RELEVANT OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS]."

Use a specific character idea or source fact to make a prompt meaningful. Describe the background briefly as a place and mood, with broad painted forms. Leave secondary scenery understated. Avoid turning unresolved choices into costume lists, decorative inventories or stacks of quality adjectives.
