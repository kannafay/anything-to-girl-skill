# Compact production prompts

Use the core prompt as a flexible starting point. Replace its slots with the current source's defining features. Include the user's explicit design choices and only the optional details useful for this image. Do not append the skill's planning steps, full example lists or a catalog of negative terms.

## Core prompt

"Create one beautiful, captivating adult female personification of [SOURCE], retaining [DISTINCTIVE COLORS AND SHAPES]. Use Genshin Impact official 2D character-illustration aesthetics with fine integrated linework, nuanced layered shading and refined facial features. Design original, fashionable, flattering, moderately sensual clothing suited to the source, choosing hairstyle, silhouette and accessories freely. Refine the face and hair, visible seams and edging, and accessory fastenings; distinguish materials through their light response. Concentrate detail around visual focal points. Graceful pose, full body with the whole head and both feet visible, quiet pale background. One figure shown once; no lettering anywhere, panels or inset views."

## Tailor it to the request

- Add chosen garment, material or accessory details when they matter to the concept. Treat wardrobe directions as optional inspiration rather than fixed outfits or required ingredients.
- For supplied images, briefly identify source/identity and style-only roles. Transfer the requested drawing qualities while retaining the single full-body composition.
- Give a focused correction for a demonstrated problem, such as unresolved fastening details or a 3D-looking face. Keep unrelated design choices open.

## Revision prompt

"Apply [REQUESTED CHANGE], preserving the adult woman's identity, source anchors and unmentioned design details. Maintain the intended illustration finish and a single text-free full-body composition with the whole head and both feet visible."
